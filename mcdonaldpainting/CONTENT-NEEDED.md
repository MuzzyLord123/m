## Blocks launch

These have to be answered before the site goes live. Most of them are five-minute answers; the certificate and the photographs are the two that need someone to go and find a file.

### Before we touch DNS: does the live site answer on www.mcdonaldpaintingcontractors.co.uk or on the bare domain, and which one is verified in Google Search Console?

**Why it matters.** Changing the canonical host on cutover is the one migration mistake that cannot be quietly undone. Whichever host the current site canonicalises to is the host the new one keeps.

*Where the answer goes:* `content/site.ts → site.url, and MIGRATION.md §3`

### Which email address should the site publish — info@mcdonaldpainting.co.uk or something on mcdonaldpaintingcontractors.co.uk? Do you own both domains, and is mail on the published one definitely being read?

**Why it matters.** The site is on one domain and the published email is on another. Procurement teams whitelist sending domains, so a quote that arrives from an address that does not match the website gets held in a spam quarantine or read as a phishing attempt. Until it is settled the site shows the phone number only.

*Where the answer goes:* `content/site.ts → email`

### What are the public liability and employers' liability limits, who is the insurer, and when does the policy renew?

**Why it matters.** Every pre-qualification questionnaire asks for these two figures and most set a minimum. Without them the compliance page says 'fully insured', which is what every decorator's site says. With them the enquiry can proceed the same day.

*Where the answer goes:* `content/site.ts → insurance`

### We need the original full-resolution photographs of the high school assembly hall, the factory work and the warehouse work — off your phone, not saved down from Instagram. Anything from the shop exterior in Willaston too.

**Why it matters.** These three jobs are the evidence that the company works in education and industry. Instagram re-encodes and crops everything it is given, and the new design runs photographs full width, so a saved-down copy will look soft. The school and factory images are also the hardest to go back and re-shoot.

*Where the answer goes:* `public/photographs/<job>/ and the matching content/projects/*.mdx`

### Can you send the current SafeContractor certificate as a PDF or photograph? We need the certificate number, what the assessment covered, and the expiry date.

**Why it matters.** It is the only third-party accreditation the company holds and it is the first thing a facilities manager or a school business manager looks for. At the moment the site says the words and shows nothing. With the certificate it becomes a page a buyer can forward to their compliance team.

*Where the answer goes:* `content/site.ts → accreditation, and public/compliance/ for the certificate image`

### We need read access to the current site — an admin login, or just permission to crawl it — before it is switched off.

**Why it matters.** The redirect map is built from the pages we can see in the navigation. An old WordPress install always has more URLs than its menu shows, and every one that is missed becomes a 404 on cutover day. This also gets the media library out at full resolution.

*Where the answer goes:* `MIGRATION.md §1 and §2`

### For the assembly hall, the Willaston shop and the factory or warehouse job: roughly when was it, who was the client (a school, a main contractor, a landlord?), how long were you on site, what was the coating system, and was the building in use while you worked?

**Why it matters.** This is what turns a photograph into evidence. A buyer reading a project record is checking one thing — have they done this in a building like mine, while it was open. Six words of scope answers it; a gallery does not.

*Where the answer goes:* `content/projects/*.mdx frontmatter`

### The current site has a testimonials page. Where did those quotes come from, and can we attribute them — at least first name, role and organisation type? Are any of them from commercial clients rather than householders?

**Why it matters.** Two commercial quotes, attributed, are worth more on this site than a dozen anonymous domestic ones. Unattributed quotes read as written in-house, which devalues the real ones sitting next to them.

*Where the answer goes:* `content/copy/testimonials.ts`

## Wanted, but not blocking

The site works without these. Each one makes it noticeably better at the job it is there to do.

### Which coating systems and manufacturers do you actually work with on steelwork, floors and roofs — and what surface preparation grades are the team set up for?

**Why it matters.** The capabilities page currently explains what these specifications involve, which is useful but generic. Naming the systems the company has applied turns it into a capability statement. Nothing is named until you name it — we are not putting a manufacturer on the site on the strength of an assumption.

*Where the answer goes:* `content/copy/capabilities.ts`

### Are the operatives who work in schools DBS checked, and does the company keep a record of it?

**Why it matters.** No school will let an unchecked adult work in an occupied building, and a school business manager will not ask twice — they will move to the next contractor. If the answer is yes it belongs on the education page in the first paragraph.

*Where the answer goes:* `content/sectors/education.mdx and content/copy/compliance.ts`

### What year did the company start trading? Someone needs to read the incorporation date on the Companies House record rather than work it back from the company number.

**Why it matters.** Years trading is one of the four figures in the data strip and it appears in the structured data. The company number suggests 2016, but a suggestion is not a source and this is the kind of detail a buyer checks.

*Where the answer goes:* `content/site.ts → founded`

### How many operatives are there, and how many separate teams can you put on site at once?

**Why it matters.** This is the question behind every enquiry from a main contractor: can this firm actually resource my job. It also decides whether a multi-building programmed contract is credible.

*Where the answer goes:* `content/site.ts → workforce`

### What address should a privacy request be sent to, and is the company registered with the Information Commissioner's Office?

**Why it matters.** The privacy notice has to say where to write. Most businesses holding customer records are required to register with the ICO — it is about £40 a year, and a procurement team checking will look for it.

*Where the answer goes:* `content/copy/privacy.ts`

### How long do you keep enquiries and quotes? A sensible answer is six years for anything that turned into a job, twelve months for the rest — but it has to be your answer.

**Why it matters.** A privacy notice that states a retention period nobody agreed to is worse than one that admits the gap. Six years is the contract limitation period, which is why it is the usual answer for quotes.

*Where the answer goes:* `content/copy/privacy.ts`

### Are there clients who would take a reference call — ideally one in education, one in industry, and one on a maintenance contract? Have they said yes to being named, or only to being phoned?

**Why it matters.** 'References on request' is on every contractor's site. 'Named references available for education and industrial contracts' is a different sentence, and it is only worth writing once it is true.

*Where the answer goes:* `content/copy/compliance.ts`

### What is the registered office address as it appears at Companies House?

**Why it matters.** A contractor's footer carries the registered name, number and office. It is also on the pre-qualification questionnaire, so it may as well be somewhere it can be copied from.

*Where the answer goes:* `content/site.ts → registeredOffice`

### Does the team hold asbestos awareness training, and IPAF or PASMA cards for access equipment? Do you own towers and MEWPs or hire them in?

**Why it matters.** Any building put up before 2000 brings the asbestos question, and industrial work is worked at height by definition. A buyer with a 1970s factory is deciding whether they will have to manage this for you.

*Where the answer goes:* `content/copy/compliance.ts`

### Is there a Google Business Profile, and do you have the login? If an agency set it up, we need it transferred rather than a second one created.

**Why it matters.** The categories on it are currently pointed at painter and decorator work. Adding commercial and industrial painting changes which searches the company shows up in on a map. A duplicate profile splits the reviews and is a nuisance to merge.

*Where the answer goes:* `MIGRATION.md §7`

### Are the M & R Painting Contractors Instagram and Facebook accounts dead? Before anything is deleted — do they still hold photographs of jobs that are not saved anywhere else?

**Why it matters.** The current site links to both sets of accounts on the same page, which reads as a company that has not finished changing its name. The links come off. The photographs behind them may be the only copies, so they get exported first.

*Where the answer goes:* `content/site.ts → socials, and MIGRATION.md §5`

### Do you hold CHAS, Constructionline, ISO 9001 or ISO 14001, or a CSCS card scheme registration? Is anything in progress?

**Why it matters.** Some frameworks will not accept a contractor without one of them. If none are held, that is fine and the site says nothing about it — but it is worth knowing which door is currently closed, and Constructionline in particular is a low bar for the work being chased.

*Where the answer goes:* `content/copy/compliance.ts`

### Do you work evenings, weekends, shutdowns and school holidays — and is there a limit on how far you will travel to do it?

**Why it matters.** Occupied buildings are the whole commercial offer. A school wants the summer holiday, a factory wants the shutdown week, a restaurant wants Sunday night to Tuesday morning. If the answer is yes it needs saying plainly on every sector page.

*Where the answer goes:* `content/copy/programmed.ts and each sector page`

### What is the VAT registration number?

**Why it matters.** It goes on the footer and on the capability statement, and it is on every pre-qualification questionnaire. Small thing, asked constantly.

*Where the answer goes:* `content/site.ts`
