Photographs go here, one directory per job:

    public/photographs/high-school-assembly-hall/01.jpg
    public/photographs/factory-and-warehouse-decoration/01.jpg
    public/photographs/shop-exterior-willaston/01.jpg

Then point at them from the matching file in content/projects/, with alt text and
the real pixel dimensions. See README.md, "Swap in a photograph".

Two things worth repeating:

1. Originals only. Do not save images down from Instagram or from the old
   WordPress site — both serve cropped, resized, re-encoded copies, and this
   design runs photographs the full width of the page.

2. If a client supplied the photograph, put their credit in the record's
   image.credit field so it prints under the image, and make sure there is
   permission to republish it. At least one of the company's Instagram posts
   credits a client account.

Until a photograph arrives, its slot renders as a labelled frame stating what
belongs there. That is deliberate. There is no stock photography anywhere on
this site.
