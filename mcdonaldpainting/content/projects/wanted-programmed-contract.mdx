The programmed maintenance page is the most valuable page on this site and it is
currently an argument without an example. One contract that is part-way through
its term would carry it — not a finished job, but a cycle: what was surveyed, what
went in year one, what is in year two, and what the phase report looks like.

What is needed: which client, the length of the term, roughly what is in each
phase, and permission to publish an anonymised version of one phase report. The
report is the exhibit. No other painting contractor in the region will be showing
one.
