sector page is written on that basis. It has no record behind it.

One job would fix that, and healthcare is the sector where a single piece of
evidence moves the needle furthest: the buyer is risk-averse, the procurement
route is formal, and no trust or practice manager appoints a contractor who has
never worked in an occupied clinical building.

was decanted or stayed in use, and whether the client will let their name be used
