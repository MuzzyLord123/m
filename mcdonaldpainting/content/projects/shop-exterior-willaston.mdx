A shopfront is a small job with a large audience. It is decorated in public, in
front of the trade the shop is trying not to lose, and the cut where paint meets
glass is the whole job as far as the owner is concerned.

The constraint is trading hours. Most of this work is done early, late or across a
closed day, with the frontage passable and the door usable at every point. Where
the shop sits in a terrace, the neighbours' frontages and the pavement in front of
them are part of the working area whether or not they are in the contract.

Willaston is a common enough name in Cheshire that the record needs to say which
one — there is one on the Wirral and one near Nantwich, and a buyer checking
whether we work in their area should not have to guess.
