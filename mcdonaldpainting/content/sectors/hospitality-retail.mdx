## The shopfront job

A small job with a large audience. It is decorated in public, in front of the
trade the shop is trying not to lose, and it is judged on two things: the line
where paint meets glass, and whether the pavement was walkable while it happened.
Where the shop sits in a terrace, the neighbours' frontages and the pavement in
front of them are part of the working area whether or not they are in the
contract.

## Why hospitality suits a programme better than most

A hotel repaints the same corridors and the same room types on a cycle, and it
already knows which months it can afford to lose rooms in. That is a programme
waiting to be written: a fixed number of room blocks a year, in the quiet months,
at a price agreed at the start of the term — instead of a scramble every time a
brand standards inspection is due.
