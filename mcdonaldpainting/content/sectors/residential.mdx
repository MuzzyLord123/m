## Why this page is not the whole website any more

The company's old website was a domestic decorator's website: colour trends, the
same two trade words repeated in every sentence for the benefit of a search
engine, and no way for a facilities manager to tell whether the firm could paint
their factory. Most of the work is now commercial and industrial, so the site
says so.

None of which changes how a house is decorated. Same preparation, same people,
same insurance, and the same person coming out to look at it.
