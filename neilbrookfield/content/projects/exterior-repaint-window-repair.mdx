Filler in a rotten sill is a two-year fix. It looks perfect the day you leave and
it goes again from the inside, and then the customer pays for the whole job a
second time. So the first day on a house like this one is not painting at all —
it is a screwdriver, every sill and every frame, finding out how much of the
timber is actually there.

Where it had gone, I cut it out until I hit wood that was sound, and let a new
piece of timber in. That is slower than filling it and it is the difference
between a paint job that lasts and one that photographs well.
