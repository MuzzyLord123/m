Write it in the first person plural — you are a team, not a man. Say what was
wrong before you say what you put on it, because that is the order the customer
experienced it in and it is the order that makes the price make sense.

Two or three short paragraphs is plenty. What was behind it when you opened it
up is always more interesting than the colour.
