The walls had been done in two colours with a dark green on the bottom half, and
it was doing what a two-tone hallway always does — chopping the wall in half at
waist height and making the whole run feel lower and narrower than it is.

Halls, stairs and landings are the hardest space in a house to work in. It is two
storeys of wall meeting over a staircase, so a good deal of the day goes on
getting safe access rather than on painting, and it is usually the darkest part of
the house before you start. It is also the one space a family cannot do without
for a week, so we sheeted it properly and kept the stairs usable the whole time we
were there.

Taking the line off the walls and running one colour from the front door to the
top landing is the single cheapest change you can make to how big a house feels.
The stairs are the same width they were in the before photograph. They do not
look it.
